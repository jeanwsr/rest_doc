# Architecture Decision Records (ADR)

An Architecture Decision Record (ADR) documents one important technical decision together with its rationale: what problem was faced at the time, which realistic alternatives existed, and why this particular choice was made. Each ADR records a single decision; once effective, it is preserved as a historical record. When a decision is taken over by a newer one, only the status is updated or the superseded parts are annotated — content is never deleted.

When to write an ADR, how ADRs differ from other documents (API documentation, developer documentation, enhancement proposals), and the authoring conventions are covered in [ADR: Rules and Explanation](adr-rules-and-explanation).
